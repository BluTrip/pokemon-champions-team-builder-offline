DATA SOURCES / ATTRIBUTION

This project is an independent offline-first front end. It retrieves and caches data from:
1. Gheist23/pokemonbattledata — Pokémon Champions battle data, builder app data, and per-Pokémon learnable-move CSVs.
   https://github.com/Gheist23/pokemonbattledata
2. tinoxo/PokeCalc — Regulation M-C roster and legality snapshots.
   https://github.com/tinoxo/PokeCalc
3. Seancheey/PokeDD — Regulation M-C additions and overrides.
   https://github.com/Seancheey/PokeDD
4. otterlyclueless/pokemon-champions-data — fallback Champions learnsets.
   https://github.com/otterlyclueless/pokemon-champions-data

Data is cached locally after the first sync. Community datasets may change; use Sync database to refresh.
